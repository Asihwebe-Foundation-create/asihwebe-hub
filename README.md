# Asihwebe Connect

Create a modern, highly trustworthy, professional, community-focused and responsive full-stack website for Asihwebe Foundation, a South African non-profit organisation focused on community development, enterprise development, digital enablement and economic inclusion.
The website must communicate that Asihwebe is a credible South African organisation working to empower entrepreneurs, small businesses, youth and underserved communities by connecting people to skills, digital tools, markets, partnerships and economic opportunities.
Brand & Visual Identity
Use a warm, professional and community-focused visual identity built around:
	• Deep navy/dark blue: #001B3F as the primary brand colour
	• Warm gold/orange accents for calls to action and highlights
	• Clean white backgrounds
	• Subtle neutral/light-grey supporting backgrounds
The overall visual style should feel credible, human, aspirational and institution-ready, suitable for engagement with government departments, development agencies, corporates, financial institutions, chambers of commerce, funders, sponsors and community organisations.
Avoid making the design feel overly corporate, overly academic, or like a generic charity website.
Use authentic-looking imagery representing:
	• South African township and rural communities
	• Entrepreneurs and small businesses
	• Youth and community development
	• Digital inclusion
	• Training and skills development
	• Local economic activity
	• People participating in workshops and enterprise programmes
Use modern Lucide icons sparingly as visual anchors.
The website must be fully responsive and optimised for desktop, tablet and mobile.

1. Navigation Bar
Create a clean, professional navigation bar with the Asihwebe Foundation logo prominently positioned.
Navigation links:
	• Home
	• About Us
	• Our Programmes
	• Partnerships
	• Impact
	• Contact
Include a prominent "Support Our Work" or "Partner With Us" call-to-action button.
The navigation should remain clean and uncluttered on mobile, with an accessible mobile menu.

2. Hero Section
Create a strong hero section with the headline:
"Empowering Communities. Enabling Opportunity. Shaping Futures."
Use the following mission-focused supporting statement:
"Asihwebe Foundation works to strengthen communities and unlock economic opportunity through enterprise development, digital enablement, skills development and strategic partnerships."
Include two prominent calls to action:
"Explore Our Programmes"
and
"Partner With Us"
The hero should communicate that Asihwebe is focused on practical, measurable community and economic impact rather than simply providing short-term assistance.

3. Who We Are / About Asihwebe
Create an engaging About section introducing Asihwebe Foundation as a South African non-profit organisation focused on community and enterprise development.
Use the following core positioning:
"Asihwebe Foundation is committed to creating pathways to participation, opportunity and sustainable economic inclusion for communities that are often underserved by traditional systems."
Explain that Asihwebe works through programmes, partnerships and practical interventions that help entrepreneurs and communities develop capabilities, adopt digital tools, access markets and connect with institutions that can support their growth.
Emphasise the Foundation's collaborative approach and its intention to work with government, corporates, development agencies, financial institutions, chambers of commerce, community organisations and other ecosystem partners.
Include a clear "Learn More About Us" button linking to the About Us page.

4. Our Programmes / Impact Areas
Create a visually engaging programme section with four primary impact areas rather than generic charity categories.
Card 1 — Enterprise Development
"Supporting entrepreneurs and small businesses with practical tools, knowledge and pathways to sustainable growth."
Card 2 — Digital Enablement
"Helping underserved entrepreneurs and communities participate in the digital economy through accessible digital tools, platforms and capability development."
Card 3 — Skills & Capacity Development
"Building practical business, digital and entrepreneurial capabilities through structured training, workshops and development programmes."
Card 4 — Community Activation & Market Access
"Connecting communities, entrepreneurs and ecosystem partners to opportunities, markets and resources that can accelerate economic participation."
Each card should have a suitable Lucide icon and a "Learn More" interaction.

5. Flagship Programme — TRDEA
Create a dedicated featured section for Asihwebe's flagship initiative:
Township & Rural Digital Enterprise Accelerator (TRDEA)
Present TRDEA as a practical enterprise-development programme designed to help township and rural entrepreneurs become more digitally enabled, market-ready and connected to economic opportunity.
Use the following programme pathway:
Registration → Business Orientation → Digital Business Profile → QR Payment Enablement → Online Marketplace Listing → Business Workshops → Market Access → Growth
Explain that the programme combines business development, digital enablement, practical workshops and market-access support.
Highlight that the programme is designed for entrepreneurs and SMEs in township and rural communities.
Include a prominent CTA:
"Explore TRDEA"
and a secondary CTA:
"Partner With TRDEA"
The design should make TRDEA feel like a scalable flagship programme rather than simply another page on the website.

6. Our Approach
Create a section explaining Asihwebe's development approach.
Use four pillars:
Connect
Connecting entrepreneurs and communities with institutions, markets, resources and opportunities.
Enable
Providing practical digital tools, business capability and skills.
Activate
Turning programmes and partnerships into practical community-level action.
Grow
Supporting entrepreneurs and communities towards sustainable participation and economic growth.
Use a simple visual flow or four-card layout.

7. Partnerships
Create a dedicated Partnerships section explaining that Asihwebe believes meaningful community impact requires collaboration across the ecosystem.
Include categories such as:
	• Government & Public Sector
	• Corporates
	• Financial Institutions
	• Development Agencies
	• Chambers & Business Organisations
	• Community Organisations
	• Training & Skills Partners
	• Technology Partners
Use the headline:
"Impact Through Partnership"
Supporting copy:
"We work with organisations that share our commitment to inclusive economic participation and sustainable community development."
Include three CTA options:
"Become a Programme Partner"
"Sponsor a Cohort"
"Collaborate With Us"
Do not invent or display partner logos unless they are subsequently supplied or specifically approved.

8. Impact Section
Create a visually compelling impact section designed to accommodate real programme statistics as Asihwebe's programmes grow.
Use initial placeholders such as:
	• Entrepreneurs Reached
	• Communities Engaged
	• Training Days Delivered
	• Digital Businesses Enabled
Clearly structure these as dynamic impact metrics so that real figures can easily be added or updated later.
Do not fabricate impact statistics.
Where appropriate, include an interactive or animated counter effect, but ensure that the underlying figures remain easy to update.

9. Why Asihwebe
Create a section communicating the Foundation's distinctive approach.
Use the following themes:
	• Community-Centred — Programmes designed around real community and entrepreneur needs.
	• Practical — Focused on implementation, capability and measurable action.
	• Digital-Enabled — Using technology to expand participation and access.
	• Partnership-Driven — Working across government, business and community ecosystems.
	• Scalable — Designing programmes that can be replicated across communities and regions.
Avoid exaggerated claims such as "transforming millions of lives" unless verified impact data is subsequently provided.

10. Support / Donation Section
Create a professional "Support Our Work" section.
Include options for:
	• Donate
	• Sponsor a Programme
	• Sponsor an Entrepreneur Cohort
	• Provide Skills or Expertise
	• Become a Strategic Partner
	• Volunteer
Create an interactive donation component where users can select preset amounts such as:
R150 | R300 | R500 | R1,000
or enter a custom amount.
For the initial version, the payment experience should be clearly labelled as a demonstration/prototype unless a real payment provider has been integrated.
Do not imply that a real payment has been processed.
The architecture should allow a real South African payment gateway to be integrated at a later stage.

11. Volunteer & Get Involved Section
Create a section encouraging individuals and organisations to participate in Asihwebe's work.
Include a functional form with:

	• Email Address

Dropdown options:
	• Volunteer
	• Provide Professional Expertise
	• Sponsor a Programme
	• Partner With Us
	• Support Entrepreneur Development
	• General Enquiry
Include a message field and a clear "Submit Enquiry" button.

14. Phakama Marketplace Relationship
Asihwebe should remain the primary brand and organisation represented by this website.
Where relevant, the website may explain that Asihwebe works with technology and ecosystem partners to support its programmes.
The website may reference Phakama Marketplace where relevant to digital enablement, marketplace access or programme implementation, but do not position Asihwebe as a subsidiary, sister organisation or division of Phakama Marketplace.
Do not make Phakama the dominant brand.
A future dedicated section can explain the relationship and role of technology partners once the exact wording has been approved.

16. Technical & UX Requirements
The website must:
	• Be fully responsive
	• Follow mobile-first principles
	• Use accessible typography and sufficient colour contrast
	• Have clear visual hierarchy
	• Use smooth but restrained animations
	• Use modern Lucide icons
	• Include hover and interaction states
	• Have accessible forms and buttons
	• Optimise images for web performance
	• Include SEO-friendly page titles and metadata
	• Use clean semantic HTML
	• Have a scalable component architecture
	• Be easy for a non-technical administrator to update later
	• Be structured so additional programmes can be added without redesigning the website
Create reusable components for:
	• Navigation
	• Hero sections
	• Programme cards
	• Impact statistics
	• CTA sections
	• Forms
	• Testimonials
	• Partner logos
	• Footer

17. Content & Design Principles
The tone should be:
Professional • Human • Credible • Aspirational • Practical • Inclusive
Avoid:
	• Generic charity clichés
	• Excessive corporate jargon
	• Unverified statistics
	• Unverified impact claims
	• Stock imagery that feels disconnected from South Africa
	• Overly dramatic poverty imagery
	• Making unsupported claims about beneficiaries
	• Presenting prototype functionality as live functionality
The website should position Asihwebe Foundation as a credible South African development organisation capable of working with institutional, corporate, government and community partners.
The overall experience should communicate:
"We understand communities, we build capability, we enable participation, and we work with partners to create practical economic opportunity."
Build the initial website with realistic placeholder content where information has not yet been supplied, but clearly identify placeholders so they can easily be replaced later.
