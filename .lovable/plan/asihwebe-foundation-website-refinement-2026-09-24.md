# Asihwebe Foundation Website Refinement

## Goal
Refine the existing website into a distinctly Asihwebe, institution-ready experience using the approved logo, positioning, programme model and South African development context. Preserve the working multi-page structure, enquiry storage and accessible interaction patterns.

## What will change
- Replace the temporary letter mark with the supplied Asihwebe logo across navigation and footer, and derive the favicon from it.
- Tighten the navy, orange and gold visual system, typography, spacing and section hierarchy without introducing decorative clutter or excessive motion.
- Refine the home page around the approved hero, institutional introduction, connected Connect–Enable–Activate–Grow journey, clear impact areas, TRDEA flagship pathway, institutional partner offer, verified-impact placeholders and broader support model.
- Strengthen the About page with the approved positioning, role-based leadership entries, Elroy Shilling’s corrected name, governance principles and expandable future board area.
- Rework Programmes to distinguish four impact areas from named programmes and present TRDEA as the flagship initiative.
- Expand the TRDEA page with the challenge, pilot design (clearly labelled as programme design, not impact), eight-step journey, programme elements, monitoring and the BGR Index™ explanation.
- Strengthen Partnerships for government, corporate, financial, development, skills, technology and community audiences, including three participation pathways and the approved institutional CTA.
- Keep the impact dashboard free of invented numbers and update its categories to the approved wording.
- Replace the donation simulation with a non-transactional Asihwebe Phakama Marketplace Account QR placeholder and broader support pathways.
- Make Contact more institutional and update its enquiry categories while retaining validation and Cloud-backed submission.
- Refine desktop and mobile navigation, including a simple programmes submenu and “Partner With Us” CTA.
- Update page titles, descriptions, Open Graph text, headings, image descriptions and internal links for the approved search themes without keyword stuffing.

## Content and credibility safeguards
- No invented statistics, biographies, LinkedIn URLs, board appointments, partner logos, certifications, outcomes or payment confirmations.
- The 100 entrepreneurs, five-day format and 20 entrepreneurs per cohort will be labelled as the current pilot programme design.
- Existing photography will be retained where it already reflects South African entrepreneurship, digital participation and collaborative learning.
- The uploaded logo will be used as supplied; no separate relationship claim about Phakama Marketplace will be added.

## Validation
- Check every public page for consistent terminology, CTA wording, metadata and heading hierarchy.
- Verify desktop and mobile navigation, key links, support QR placeholder, contact categories and enquiry submission.
- Confirm the latest preview build and runtime diagnostics are clean.
